# Roadmap after Foundation v1

Foundation v1 ships only Decay, Nuclide Atlas, Detector, and Pulse. The following are candidates, not current commitments.

1. **Count Lab** — live counting statistics, dead-time concepts, background subtraction as a clearly bounded teaching model.
2. **Cloud Lab** — cloud chambers and event tracks, starting with archived or synthetic observations rather than live hardware control.
3. **Binding Lab** — mass defect, binding energy, and nuclear stability patterns.
4. **Quantum collection** — state evolution, interference, and measurement without decorative “quantum” metaphors.
5. **Astronomy collection** — orbital mechanics, light curves, spectra, and scale.
6. **Chemistry and materials collections** — reaction networks, phase behavior, transport, and microstructure.
7. **Phenomena Studio** — an authoring surface only after manifests, accessibility, data provenance, and model-review workflows have been exercised by multiple internal Labs.

Any expansion must preserve device-local core interaction, inspectable assumptions, provenance, bilingual accessibility, and product safety boundaries.
