# Roadmap

Foundation v1 is intentionally limited to the Shell and four nuclear Labs. The following are candidates, not commitments for this release:

## After Foundation

- Count Lab: counting statistics, sampling, and the shape of uncertainty.
- Cloud Lab: tracks, droplets, and the visible consequences of invisible interactions.
- Binding Lab: a tactile view of mass defect and nuclear binding energy.
- Quantum collection: state, measurement, and interference without pretending to be a universal quantum simulator.
- Astronomy collection: light, spectra, orbital motion, and scale.
- Chemistry collection: reaction rates, equilibrium, and molecular structure.
- Studio: authoring, provenance checks, scenario composition, and a reviewable Lab package format.

## Explicitly out of scope for Foundation v1

No general plugin marketplace, monorepo migration, paid API, account system, database, real-time collaboration, research-grade nuclear analysis, dosimetry, safety recommendation, or regulatory workflow is introduced here.
